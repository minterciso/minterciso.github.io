Asterisk infrastructure rebuild

Rebuilt the whole company's telephony with 6 hours of downtime, starting from close to zero telephony knowledge.

Period
2013
Organisation
Método Engenharia
Role
DBA / Network Administrator

Stack

  • Asterisk
  • Linux
  • E1
  • Jabber

Tags

  • technical
  • infrastructure
  • linux

What it was

With little to no knowledge of telephony infrastructure, I was asked to rebuild the Asterisk infrastructure for the entire company, with a window of around 8 hours of downtime.

What I did

We finished in 6 hours, having:

  • created the Asterisk server
  • configured the E1 line
  • updated and migrated the Jabber service in use at the time
  • configured every extension via UDP deploy

Challenges

Needless to say this was a challenging task, but with the right people to ask and the right team to support the more repetitive parts, it turned into a successful piece of work — the kind worth telling at a bar.

Outcome

Solved for good. Whatever had prompted the rebuild never happened again.

The switchover landed inside the agreed window — 6 hours out of 8 — and the company’s telephony stayed stable from then on, with no recurrence.
