What it was

A GenAI assistant for a call centre operation, designed to support level 1 analysts in resolving tickets and so reduce the volume escalated to level 2.

The project came out of the Disruption Office, a cross-firm unit created to standardise the GenAI demand arriving at Deloitte Brazil and to build reusable assets for other business units. I joined it at its formation.

What I did

I was architect and lead developer on a 3-person squad, owning technical direction and lifecycle management for the asset — from architecture through to production, not just to handover.

Outcome

The assistant went into production supporting level 1 analysts in resolving tickets and reducing escalation to level 2.
